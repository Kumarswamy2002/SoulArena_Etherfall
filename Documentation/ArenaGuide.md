## Dynamic Stage Phases
All arenas progress through 3 distinct dynamic phases over a 99-second match:
- **Phase 1 (0s - 30s) — Neutral Arena**: Standard arena topology and pristine destructibles.
- **Phase 2 (30s - 60s) — Environmental Hazard Surge**: Magma pools flare, ice slippery friction increases, and gust currents intensify.
- **Phase 3 (60s+ / Sudden Death) — Stage Boundary Collapse**: Outer boundaries collapse inwards, shrinking combat space and forcing decisive close-quarters engagements.
